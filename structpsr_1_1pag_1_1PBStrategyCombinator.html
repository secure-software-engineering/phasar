Detailed Description

template<PBStrategy FirstT, CanOnAddEdgeN< typename FirstT::n_t > SecondT>
struct psr::pag::PBStrategyCombinator< FirstT, SecondT >

Fans out every PAG-builder callback to two independent strategies. FirstT must be a full PBStrategy; SecondT only needs to satisfy CanOnAddEdge (it borrows the withCalleesOfCallAt and analysis-domain types from FirstT).

Useful for intersecting alias analyses.
