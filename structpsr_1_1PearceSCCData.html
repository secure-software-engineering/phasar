Detailed Description

template<typename GraphNodeId>
struct psr::PearceSCCData< GraphNodeId >

State of Pearce's SCC algorithm. Can be reused across multiple calls to visitSCCsFrom() on the same graph and, after reset(), on other graphs to avoid repeated allocations.
