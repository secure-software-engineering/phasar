Detailed Description

template<CanOnAddEdge FirstT, PBStrategyBase SecondT>
struct psr::pag::PBMixin< FirstT, SecondT >

Upgrades a CanOnAddEdge type to a full PBStrategy by mixing in the analysis-domain types and withCalleesOfCallAt from a separate PBStrategyBase. Typical use: wrap an edge-listener (e.g., a union-find accumulator) that does not own a call-graph with a call-graph provider.

Template Parameters
FirstTThe edge-listener. Must satisfy CanOnAddEdge.
SecondTThe call-graph / domain provider. Must satisfy PBStrategyBase.
