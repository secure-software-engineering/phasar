Detailed Description

Debug-only PAG-builder strategy that emits the PAG as a Graphviz DOT digraph to llvm::errs(). The opening "digraph PAG {" is printed in the constructor and the closing brace in the destructor (move-construction transfers ownership, preventing a double-close).
