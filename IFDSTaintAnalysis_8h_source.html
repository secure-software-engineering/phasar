35/**
36 * This analysis tracks data-flows through a program. Data flows from
37 * dedicated source functions, which generate tainted values, into
38 * dedicated sink functions. A leak is reported once a tainted value
39 * reached a sink function.
40 *
41 * @see LLVMTaintConfig on how to specify your own
42 * taint-sensitive source and sink functions.
43 */
