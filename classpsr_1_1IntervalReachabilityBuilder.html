Detailed Description

template<SmallIdType VtxId>
class psr::IntervalReachabilityBuilder< VtxId >

Computes an IntervalReachabilityIndex during a single DFS (Pearce's SCC algorithm, see visitSCCsFrom()).

Owns scratch buffers that are reused across build() calls; keep the builder alive when indexing many (small) graphs.

Complexity: O(V + E + total size of the merged interval sets).
